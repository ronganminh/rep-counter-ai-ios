Delete your data

The current version does not provide user accounts. Delete workout history through Settings → Delete local history, clear the app's data in Android settings, or uninstall the app.

Camera permission

Camera access is required for the core pose-detection and rep-counting experience. You can revoke this permission at any time through Android's app settings.

AI feedback issues

AI feedback requires an internet connection and may occasionally be unavailable or inaccurate. Camera images and videos are not included in AI requests.

Response time

We aim to respond to support emails as soon as reasonably possible. Support is currently provided in English and Vietnamese.
