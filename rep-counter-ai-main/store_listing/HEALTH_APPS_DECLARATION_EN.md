# Google Play Health Apps Declaration — Suggested Answers

## App category

Select:

- **Activity and fitness**
- Exercise / workout tracking, if Play Console presents a more specific option

Do not select medical-device, diagnosis, treatment, clinical decision support, or disease-management categories.

## Feature description

`RepCoach AI uses the device camera and on-device MediaPipe pose estimation to count push-up repetitions, help users position themselves in view, record workout goals and history locally, and optionally generate general fitness feedback from a lightweight workout summary. The app does not upload camera frames or videos and is not a medical device.`

## Required disclaimer

Use this wording in the store listing and wherever Play Console requests an explanation:

`RepCoach AI is a general fitness tool, not a medical device. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, cure, or prevention of any medical condition. AI feedback and repetition counts may be inaccurate. Users should stop exercising if they experience pain, dizziness, or unusual discomfort and should consult a qualified healthcare professional when appropriate.`

## Camera permission justification

`Camera access is required only for the core push-up counting experience. Video frames are analyzed on the device with MediaPipe and are not uploaded by the app. Users see a clear explanation before the Android camera permission prompt.`

## Content-rating guidance

- No violence, sexual content, gambling, controlled substances, or user-generated public content in the current MVP.
- Choose the truthful age group for your intended audience. A conservative launch choice is **13 and older** unless the product is specifically redesigned and reviewed for children.
- Answer that the app provides fitness guidance, but not medical advice or medical treatment.
