## Full description

Train with more structure using RepCoach AI, a camera-based push-up counter that runs pose detection directly on your device.

DEVICE REQUIREMENTS

Rep counting requires a compatible Android phone with a working front-facing camera and enough space to place the full body in view. Accuracy may vary with camera angle, lighting, clothing, visibility, and device performance.

SET A GOAL AND START MOVING

Choose your target before each session or skip the goal and train freely. RepCoach AI counts completed repetitions and celebrates when you reach your target.

REAL-TIME POSE GUIDANCE

Use the front camera and follow the on-screen body guide to position yourself. MediaPipe analyzes body landmarks on your device to count push-ups and provide useful form signals while you train.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Camera frames and videos you select for testing are processed on your device. They are not uploaded by the app. Workout history is stored locally and can be deleted at any time.

OPTIONAL AI FEEDBACK

After a workout, you can choose to send a lightweight session summary—such as rep count, duration, goal, and movement-quality metrics—to our secure service and Google Gemini. No camera images or videos are included. AI feedback may be inaccurate and should be treated as general fitness guidance.

TRACK YOUR PROGRESS

Review previous sessions, see whether you reached your goal, and keep building a consistent routine.

Current features:

- On-device push-up counting with MediaPipe
- Front-camera positioning guide
- Optional rep goals and goal celebration
- Local workout history
- Optional AI-generated session feedback
- Vietnamese and English interface
