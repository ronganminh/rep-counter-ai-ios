| L-D17 | F-4 drafts and D-06 window | Drafts are recorded **from `WorkoutUiState` only** by a listener created in `CameraPage` (no change to `WorkoutController`, `RepCounter`/`RepTracker` or the HUD). Recovery window = **12 h** (D-06 PROPOSED value), a constant. Expired drafts are still offered as Save partial / Discard, never discarded silently. "Save as partial" writes one record with a fixed id (`draft-<start>`, `partial: true`, no form data); a draft whose workout was already saved is cleared instead of offered. **Resume** (owner decision B, 2026-10-26, a controlled engine exception): inside the window, free and target-reps drafts (not timed, not routine) can be resumed. `WorkoutController` takes a `WorkoutResumeSeed` (reps, sets, time, start, record id) and adds it to what a fresh `SessionTracker` counts; `RepCounter` (Schmitt trigger) and `RepTracker` are unchanged and not seeded. | L-D19 | PL-1 multi-day plans (owner decision 2026-10-26, PRODUCT_DECISIONS PL-1) | **Model + rules (PL-1a):** `WorkoutPlan` (id, ownerId null for guests, name, goal, difficulty, weekdays, templates, timezone, startDate, status draft/active/paused/completed/archived, version, day overrides, `progressiveOverload` = false) and `PlannedSession` (id, planId, local date, template id, copied targets, state scheduled/completed/partial/missed/skipped/cancelled, linked WorkoutRecord ids, original date, rescheduledFrom, append-only history) stored locally as one JSON document (`workout_plans_v1`), separate from WorkoutRecords (never edited). Dates are local calendar dates; "today" is the device's local day; the plan keeps the device time-zone label. Sessions are generated four weeks ahead with an A/B/C rotation fixed by the calendar. A session becomes missed only after its local day ends with nothing linked; it is never moved automatically. Saved workouts link by id to the session on their own day whose targets include the exercise (idempotent: a record is linked once; sync retries add nothing); state is completed when recorded reps/time meet every target, otherwise partial; a partial record keeps it partial; manual reps count but stay marked manual. Rescheduling is explicit, keeps the session id, original date and history; an occupied day is refused (no silent overwrite; **the Phase 2 "two workouts in one day after confirm" is not offered**); a recovery day needs explicit confirmation and then becomes a training day; alternatives are the nearest free days. Editing the plan replans only untouched future sessions. Pausing misses nothing; resuming cancels the paused days. **Default templates use only engine exercises** (push-up, pull-up, curl, overhead extension; D-28): Full Body A/B/C have no legs/core moves until engine profiles exist. Owner spec wins over the Phase 2 prototype on: Mon/Wed/Fri default (prototype 4 days), 2–6 sessions (prototype 2–5), overload toggle (not in designs) | Owner decision; local-first, no plan endpoint exists (API gap analysis: plans need no backend) | Revert the PL-1 PRs; `workout_plans_v1` is only read by the plan feature | Product (template content: Product + coach review) |
